## Promo Strategy Tips

### Best Practices:
- **Announce 3-5 days in advance** via email/social media
- **Highlight specific products** to drive interest in underperforming SKUs
- **Create urgency** with countdown timers
- **Bundle deals** encourage larger orders
- **Seasonal themes** make promos more memorable

### Product Rotation Ideas:
1. **Week 1 of month:** General sitewide discount (15-20%)
2. **Week 3 of month:** Category-specific spotlight (20-25% on selected peptides)

### Special Event Promos:
- New product launches: 20% off for first 2 weeks
- Customer appreciation: Random surprise discounts
- Bulk research orders: Tiered discounts (10%/15%/20% at $300/$500/$750)
- Referral rewards: 15% off for both referrer and referee

### Email Subject Line Ideas:
- "🔬 Research Alert: [Product] 20% Off This Week Only"
- "⚡ Flash Sale: 24 Hours to Save Big on Research Peptides"
- "🎯 Spotlight Sale: Premium [Category] Peptides on Sale"
- "💰 Double Discount Days: Stack Your Savings"
